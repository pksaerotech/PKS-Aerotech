01 / WHO WE ARE

Big ambitions.
Built from the ground up.

PKS Aerotech is an emerging aerospace technology startup based in Karnataka, India, focused on the research and development of unmanned aerial systems.

Our current focus is the development of a VTOL delivery drone platform. We are exploring how mechanical design, electric propulsion, embedded electronics, flight control and systems integration can work together to support next-generation aerial logistics.

We believe meaningful innovation begins with hands-on engineering, rigorous testing and people who are curious enough to build what comes next.

03 / DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Engineering is
a process.

Our development approach emphasizes feasibility, disciplined design and safe testing. These are planned phases, not completed milestones.

PHASE 01

Research & Feasibility

Define mission requirements, review aircraft concepts, and evaluate design constraints.

PHASE 02

Concept Design & Simulation

Develop initial CAD models, propulsion estimates, electrical architecture and simulations.

PHASE 03

Prototype Development

Integrate selected components and perform controlled bench and ground validation.

PHASE 04

Testing & Iteration

Plan compliant flight testing where authorized, measure performance and refine the design.
