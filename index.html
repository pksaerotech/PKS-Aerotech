About PKS Aerotech

PKS Aerotech is a Karnataka-based startup focused on developing practical and affordable technology solutions for agriculture and rural industries.

We are working on smart agriculture services and future product development in areas such as agricultural drones, robotics, IoT-based farm monitoring, EV farm vehicles, automation, 3D printing, crop monitoring, mapping and AI-based farming support.

Our mission is to understand real farmer problems and build useful technology that saves time, reduces labour dependency, improves productivity and supports the future of Indian agriculture.

Our Solutions

Agricultural Drone Services
Drone spraying, crop monitoring, farm survey and mapping.

Smart Farming & IoT
Soil monitoring, irrigation alerts, farm automation and sensor-based solutions.

Robotics & EV Farm Vehicles
Future development of robotic and electric farm support vehicles for farmers.

3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping
Custom parts, drone components, farm tool prototypes and product development support.

AI-Based Agriculture Support
Future crop analytics, decision support, pest observation and farm data solutions.

Student Research Program

PKS Aerotech is inviting students to participate in farmer field research to understand real agricultural problems and identify future technology solutions.

Selected students will interview farmers directly on agricultural land, collect genuine field insights, and help us understand what products or services should be built for farmers.

First batch: 10 students only
Preferred location: Bangalore Rural
Interview languages: Kannada / Telugu
Opportunity: Top-performing students may be considered for internship opportunities.

Our Vision

To become one of India’s leading agriculture technology companies by building innovative, affordable and practical solutions for farmers.

Our long-term vision is to develop indigenous technology in drones, robotics, IoT, EV farm systems, automation and AI-powered rural innovation.

PKS Aerotech aims to create technology that is not only advanced, but also useful, accessible and trusted by farmers.
